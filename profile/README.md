## 🚀 Overview
Rosetta Stone for Windows helps you build practical language skills by surrounding you with the language you want to learn instead of relying on translation. The Rosetta Stone app connects words to images and real situations, so you pick up vocabulary and grammar the way you learned your first language. With a wide range of Rosetta Stone languages available — including Rosetta Stone Spanish — it suits complete beginners and returning learners who want a steady, structured path to conversation.

## ✨ Features
- **Immersive lessons** — Learn through pictures, native audio, and context rather than long grammar tables.
- **Speech recognition** — Practice pronunciation out loud and get instant feedback that helps you sound clearer.
- **Many languages** — Choose from a broad catalog of Rosetta Stone languages and switch focus whenever you like.
- **Structured path** — Move through units that build steadily from first words to everyday conversation.
- **Offline lessons** — Download selected lessons so you can keep learning without a constant connection.

## 🎯 Getting Started
1. Open Rosetta Stone and pick the language you want to study.
2. Set a comfortable daily goal and choose your starting level.
3. Work through your first immersive lesson, tapping images as prompts appear.
4. Use the speech practice to say new words and phrases aloud.
5. Return each day to continue your path and review earlier material.

## ❓ FAQ
**Is Rosetta Stone free?**
Rosetta Stone is paid software offered by subscription, with a lifetime option that unlocks all languages. A free trial is available so you can try the lessons before you decide to subscribe.

**Which versions of Windows are supported?**
Rosetta Stone runs on current 64-bit versions of Windows, including Windows 10 and Windows 11. Keeping Windows updated gives you the smoothest experience.

**Is my data secure?**
Your account and learning progress are protected with standard account security. For full details, review the privacy information provided with the app and on the official website.
